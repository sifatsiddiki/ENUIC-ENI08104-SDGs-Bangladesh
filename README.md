# ENI08104: English and Skills for Advanced Study (ENUIC)
## Presentation: Challenges in Implementing UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) in Bangladesh

This repository contains the academic presentation and research materials developed for the course **ENI08104** during the **2024-5 TR1** trimester at **Edinburgh Napier University International College (ENUIC)**, presented on **4 September 2025**.

###  Project Focus
The research critically analyzes the specific socio-economic and geographical challenges faced by Bangladesh—a developing nation highly vulnerable to climate change—in achieving the United Nations 2030 SDG agenda. It specifically highlights critical goals including **SDG 1 (No Poverty)**, **SDG 3 (Good Health)**, **SDG 4 (Quality Education)**, **SDG 5 (Gender Equality)**, and **SDG 13 (Climate Action)**.

###  Key Insights Covered
* **The Core Dilemma:** Balancing rapid economic development with extreme climate vulnerability (flooding, cyclones, sea-level rise) and overpopulation.
* **Statistical Positioning:** Analysis of Bangladesh's SDG ranking (107th out of 193 states with a score of 64.35).
* **Strategic Solutions:** Exploring Public-Private Partnerships (PPP), Green Infrastructure, Community-Led Development, and Policy Reforms based on global frameworks.

###  Academic Framework & References
The presentation incorporates academic literature and structures solutions using standard citation practices, referencing frameworks from:
* Morton et al. (2017) - Governance & Policy
* Stewart (2015) - Community-Led Development
* Australian SDG Implementation Report (2018) - Public-Private Partnerships
* United Nations Bangladesh & The Daily Star reports
